# Upstream and attribution

Notch is a community fork and derivative work of [Boring Notch](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch),
created and maintained by The Bored Team and its contributors.

The fork retains substantial upstream work, including the macOS notch and
window architecture, settings surfaces, gestures, media integrations, helper
targets, and the surrounding application structure. Those foundations are
not original to this fork and remain credited to the upstream project.

This repository adds focused Codex quota, pace, local cost-estimate, reset
forecast, and related presentation/configuration work. The added features are
intended to be a small layer on top of Boring Notch, not a claim that the
underlying notch application was created here.

Notch is an independent community build. It is not an official Boring Notch
release, is not endorsed by The Bored Team, and should not be confused with
the upstream project. For the original application, releases, issues, and
contribution guidance, visit [TheBoredTeam/boring.notch](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch).

License and notices from the upstream project and other dependencies are
preserved in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES`](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES).
